# Myria Consulting — Google Search visibility and earned-authority checklist

## Scope
This checklist distinguishes code changes from actions that require access to a Google account or action by a third-party publisher. Do not claim ranking improvements, reviews, backlinks or Google indexing without evidence.

## Search Console — property owner actions
1. In Google Search Console, create or open a **Domain** property for `myriaconsulting.com`; verify via the DNS TXT record provided by Google (do not commit DNS tokens).
2. Submit `https://myriaconsulting.com/sitemap.xml` after deployment.
3. Use URL Inspection for the homepage, `/services`, each of the six `/services/[slug]` pages, and original Insights articles. Record whether the canonical is selected and whether Google indexes each page.
4. Inspect **Page indexing** exclusions, **Core Web Vitals**, and **Performance** for queries, pages and countries. Export a 28-day baseline and review after 28 and 90 days.
5. Validate the deployed `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, JSON-LD and page metadata using HTTP and rich-results testing. Neither a sitemap nor a request for indexing guarantees inclusion.

## Professional recognition and earned links — editorial process
- Keep organization name, URL, services and location consistent across professional profiles.
- Consider eligible Canadian consulting associations, local business organizations, Clutch, Consulting.ca and reputable industry directories. Follow each publisher's eligibility, review and payment rules.
- Pitch original Myria research, practical frameworks and business transformation perspectives to trade journals and professional associations. A published article or a listing **may** produce a link; neither is guaranteed.
- Do not fabricate client testimonials, project case studies, awards, memberships, partners or published coverage.
- Avoid paid link schemes, mass-directory submission, reciprocal link exchanges and misleading structured-data ratings.

## Measurement
Track branded versus non-branded impressions, indexed service URLs, organic consultation inquiries, earned referring domains, and top query/page combinations. Compare trends, not assumed rankings.
